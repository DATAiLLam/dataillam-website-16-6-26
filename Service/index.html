COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently
Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about how we work. Can't find an answer? Reach out directly.

How quickly will I see results? +

The GTM Engine is built and activated within 12 weeks. You'll have a live Pipeline, a running Outreach System, and structured BD Operations by the end of Week 9. Revenue Outcomes depend on your Deal Cycles, but the Infrastructure that drives them is fully operational within the Engagement.

Will your team work inside our existing processes or replace them? +

We embed alongside your Team, not over it. We Audit what exists, keep what works, fix what doesn't, and build what's missing. Your Team stays in the loop - we just bring the Architecture and Execution Discipline they've been missing.

What's the minimum commitment to get started? +

BDaaS Launch is a fixed 12-week Engagement. BDaaS Scale runs on a Monthly Retainer with a minimum 6-month Commitment. We don't do one-off Projects or short Sprints because BD Infrastructure takes time to build and govern correctly.

What happens after the engagement ends? +

The Systems, Processes, and Pipeline we build stay with you. We document everything - your GTM Framework, Sales Architecture, and Outreach Playbooks. If you want continued Execution and Governance beyond the initial Engagement, we structure an ongoing Retainer. You're never left with a Handover Deck and no Support.
